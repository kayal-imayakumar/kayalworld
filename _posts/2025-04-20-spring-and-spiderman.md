Hi friends, It’s been a long time since I posted on my blog. I’m here to show you one of the greatest art works I have ever done.

The media I used to draw this was **Charcoal.** Did you know that Charcoal can also be a medicine? Doctors use it to treat to remove poison from human bodies. That doesn’t mean you should eat charcoal pencils!!

On the face, between the webs if you look closely, you’ll see little marks. Those are called hatching lines, and they are short like hair on a male human head.
