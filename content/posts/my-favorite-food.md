Today, I am posting my own hand-drawn picture of my favorite food, [Dosai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dosa_(food)) and [Tomato Chutney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomato_chutney)

My mom makes really yummy tomato chutney and my dad makes really really crispy dosais that I love 💖 to eat.

![](/assets/images/my-favorite-food/431e2591-cf36-48d7-8b95-b364bbfce695.jpeg)

In the drawing, I used a big plate to draw the border and a small plate for the inner circle. I used color pencils to color the dosai and the tomato chutney. It was really simple, but I enjoyed doing it.
