This is my Leprechaun invitation to come to our house and give us his pot of gold so we can be **RICH 🌟💰.** I made this invitation to be nice to the Leprechaun and convince him to come to our house and give us the gold.

Did you notice the double colored heart? I drew it by taping two colored pencils together and then drawing with them. I came up with this idea myself and I am proud of this innovation.

Below is the backside of the invitation. You can see that I drew a lucky four leaf clover 🍀 and a rainbow for good luck.

**Here's a fact:** *If you don't wear green on St.Patrick's Day, the Leprechaun will pinch🤏🏼 you.*

Be aware ⚠️ of the Leprechauns. They can mess up your house.
