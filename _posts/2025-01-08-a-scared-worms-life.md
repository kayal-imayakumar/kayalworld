Happy New Year ✨ 🥳 !!

Today, I am coming to you with an intriguing under water scene. As we swim down into the unknown I thought I might introduce you to the living things in this picture. The fish you see here is a clownfish and the scared worm is currently on a hook, waiting for its demise 😥.

The medias I used were, color pencils for the worm and the hook, oil pastel entirely for the clownfish, and for the background/water I used water color. You can see how the orange and yellow colors on the fish blend in together. Even though clownfish seem funny, this one isn’t! He’s hangry!
